Random Forest Definition:
    Random Forest is an ensemble machine learning algorithm that combines many Decision Trees
     and uses their combined predictions to make a more accurate and robust prediction.

in decision trees , there is overfitting issue because one tree might learn the training data
    to specially.

Decision Trees are highly prone to overfitting because they can keep splitting 
        the data until they almost memorize the training dataset.

There is a high chances of overfitting when data is complex , bcz trees getting splitted and learns
    training data to specially.

Random forest solves this by creating many different trees instead of relying on just one.

There are two ensembling techniques in random forest:(ensemble : combining models together)
    they are : 1) Bagging
               2) Boosting

Bagging: B stands for bootstrap and agging stands for aggrigation
 Bootstrap mean sampling with replacement
 Step1: Sampling with replacement
    based on dataset we take samples , ex : n_samples = 5
    then it takes 5 random rows for each sample. and without replacement mean
    a row may occur twice or more and a row never occurs so each sampling has differnt entropy values.
step 2 : Decision trees are build based on each sample.
step 3 : test data passes to each tree and computes the majority one. This is known as aggregation.
